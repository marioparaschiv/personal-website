## Solution

Telecord is a unified messaging hub that brings Discord and Telegram together into a single, real-time feed. Built for users who manage multiple accounts and communities across both platforms, it eliminates constant app-switching by combining messages, media, and notifications into one streamlined interface.

## Key Features

- **Unified Message Feed** — View conversations from Discord and Telegram side by side in a single, real-time stream
- **Multi-Channel Broadcasting** — Send messages across multiple channels simultaneously with smart queue management and duplicate detection
- **AI-Assisted Rewriting** — Rewrite messages on the fly with AI to match tone, format, or language requirements
- **Contract & Wallet Detection** — Automatically detect cryptocurrency contract addresses and wallet mentions with integrated trading terminal support
- **Keyword Highlighting** — Customize keyword alerts to surface the messages that matter most in high-volume channels
- **Advanced Filtering** — Filter messages by platform, channel, keyword, or sender to cut through the noise
