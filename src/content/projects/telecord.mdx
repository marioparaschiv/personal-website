If you're managing communities on both Discord and Telegram, you're switching between apps constantly. Telecord puts both into one real-time feed so you can stop tabbing back and forth.

It handles multi-channel broadcasting with deduplication, has AI-assisted message rewriting for adjusting tone or language, and lets you set keyword alerts to catch what matters in busy channels.
