## Solution

Unbound is an open-source modification for the Discord mobile app that gives users unprecedented control over their experience. With a write-once, run-everywhere approach, it delivers plug-ins, themes, fonts, and icon packs across all platforms — letting you make Discord truly yours.

## Key Features

- **Plugin System** — Install and manage community-built plug-ins that extend Discord's functionality far beyond what's available out of the box
- **Custom Theming** — Apply and switch between themes that completely transform the look and feel of the app
- **Font & Icon Packs** — Swap out default fonts and icons with custom packs for a fully personalized interface
- **Cross-Platform** — Works consistently across iOS and Android with a single codebase, so your setup follows you everywhere
